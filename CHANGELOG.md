# What's new

Newest first. `/update` shows members the entries since their version.

## 0.9.1: memes and GIFs
- Ask for a meme on any line ("the Schitt's Creek David 'ew' meme here, with my text"). The Lab finds it, picks the best match and pops it in, styled in your look. Say "meme 3" to swap or "full screen" for a cutaway.
- Your own GIFs and videos work too: "use this GIF".
- Your design file now also sets your icon style: line weight, rounded or square, and tile colours.

## 0.9.0: beta
- First beta: edit talking-head reels by chatting. Rough cut, review in chat, automatic build in your look.
- Four starter looks (Editorial, Playful, Minimal, Bold), all changeable ("use these colours instead"), or your own from your brand.
- Hooks: 3 options per reel. Say "hook 2" to swap.
- Pop-up graphics that illustrate what you say: chips, numbers, chat windows, checklists and more.
- Sound sets (soft, clicky, playful, punchy) and a sound menu.
- HDR iPhone clips are converted automatically. Vertical clips up to about 4 minutes.
