# What's new

Newest first. `/update` shows members the entries since their version.

## 0.9.3: studio polish
- Each look now has a colour grade (warm, editorial or moody) and soft gradients that keep text readable.
- Close-ups and punch-ins are cut from your 4K original, so they stay sharp. Graphics move with your face when it's closer.
- New looks: Moody (a dark editorial studio) and Scrapbook (retro-editorial collage).
- New motion: blur-pop and letter-by-letter rise.
- Your own reels can orbit around you. Drop 6 of your reels in once.
- An Instagram profile follow animation for calls to action, with your real numbers.
- Another creator's reel as a card, always credited with their @handle.
- Custom animated inserts, like an app you use recreated on screen.
- Every build makes a cut check and a small phone copy for quick review.

## 0.9.2: posting quality
- Several clips for one reel: filmed it in parts or in a few takes? Drop them all in and say "these clips". The Lab picks the best take of each line across all of them.
- Your reels now come out at Instagram's volume, with the sound cleaned up a little. Before, they went out as quiet as your phone recorded them.
- Nothing sits under Instagram's buttons any more. Captions, hooks, labels and pop-ups stay clear of the username, caption and like / comment / share buttons, and every build checks it.
- Names come out right in captions ("Claude", not "cloud"). The Lab remembers the names you use.

## 0.9.1: memes and GIFs
- Ask for a meme on any line ("the Schitt's Creek David 'ew' meme here, with my text"). The Lab finds it, picks the best match and pops it in, styled in your look. Say "meme 3" to swap or "full screen" for a cutaway.
- Your own GIFs and videos work too: "use this GIF".
- Your design file now also sets your icon style: line weight, rounded or square, and tile colours.

## 0.9.0: beta
- First beta: edit talking-head reels by chatting. Rough cut, review in chat, automatic build in your look.
- Four starter looks (Editorial, Playful, Minimal, Bold), all changeable ("use these colours instead"), or your own from your brand.
- Hooks: 3 options per reel. Say "hook 2" to swap.
- Pop-up graphics that illustrate what you say: chips, numbers, chat windows, checklists and more.
- Sound sets (soft, clicky, playful, punchy) and a sound menu.
- HDR iPhone clips are converted automatically. Vertical clips up to about 4 minutes.
