# Sound effects

All generated by tools/make_sfx.py: original, free to use and share.
Numbers match sfx/sound-menu.mp4. Sound sets (which sounds go on which moment) are in sfx/palettes.json.

1. **pop**: hook lands, a point appears
2. **soft_pop**: highlighted word, subtle
3. **bubble**: playful pop-in
4. **tick**: label, small UI moment
5. **mouse_click**: a click, a choice, 'just tap this'
6. **keyboard**: typing a prompt, writing
7. **shutter**: a snapshot, a screenshot, freeze moment
8. **soft_swoosh**: gentle transition
9. **woosh**: bigger transition, into a statement
10. **swipe**: quick swipe, next idea
11. **riser**: build-up before a reveal
12. **thud**: a heavy point lands, a statement slams in
13. **ding**: an aha, a correct answer
14. **sparkle**: magic, transformation, a win
15. **blip**: notification, a new message
16. **page_flip**: next chapter, a list item
