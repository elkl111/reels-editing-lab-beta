# Reels Editing Lab: getting started

You do this once. After that you just talk to Claude.

## You need
- A Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer)
- A paid Claude plan and the Claude desktop app
- About 6 GB of free space
- 10 minutes

## Steps
1. Open the Claude desktop app, go to the **Code** tab, and choose this folder.
2. Say **"set me up"**. Claude:
   - installs what's missing and downloads the speech model, which runs on your Mac (nothing gets uploaded)
   - asks you 3 quick questions about your style
   - shows you the looks on a clip of yours, so you can pick one
3. Then drop a clip into the `inbox` folder, or into the chat, and say **"edit this reel"**.
